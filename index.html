The question we hear most

Yes. Here is exactly what happens during your appointment.

  1. 01

    Your eyes stay closed the whole time.

  2. 02

    Protective pads sit under your eyes, so the light never shines into them.

    The light cures the glue in seconds. That is why the set feels lighter and holds longer.

Questions

Before you book.

How long does a Russian manicure really last?

Three to four weeks for most clients. The cuticle is cleaned with a fine machine bit, so the color can sit right against the skin and has nothing to lift from.

Will the machine damage my nails?

No. We file gently and never thin the nail itself. If your nails are already weak, start with a Japanese manicure. It strengthens them from the inside.

My new microshading looks dark. Is that normal?

Yes. Brows look darker for the first few days, then soften by about 40 to 50 percent as they heal. The retouch after one month sets the final shade. Book it within two months of your first session.

How often do lashes need a refill?

Every two to three weeks. Refills are possible up to three weeks after your set.

Can you remove work from another salon?

Yes. Removing gel polish or BIAB costs 50 MAD with a new treatment, or 100 MAD on its own. Hard gel is 100 MAD with a new treatment, or 150 MAD on its own. Lash removal is 100 to 200 MAD.
